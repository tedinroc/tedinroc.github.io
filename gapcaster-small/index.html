Audio description that fits the silences.

GapCaster Small drafts a timed, spoken audio-description track on one laptop. It finds the silences between dialogue, writes a line for each, speaks and measures it, shortens what overflows, and shows the visual evidence behind every line so a human describer approves what is released. Nothing leaves the machine.

Six stages, all local — plus a browser studio for describers

No frontier API, no cloud upload, no account. Four of the six stages use deterministic code or tiny speech models under 1B; only visual facts, short-line drafting and checking call Qwen3-VL-8B.

01

Map the silences

Whisper large-v3-turbo (0.8B) word timestamps and Silero VAD independently mark dialogue. Silences keep 0.25 s lead and 0.30 s tail; long silences split at PySceneDetect shot cuts.

02

Read the shots

Qwen3-VL-8B returns JSON facts per shot: who, where, action, on-screen text, key visual fact and confidence. A describer-written cast sheet controls how characters are named.

03

Write to budget

Drafts are budgeted at 2.6 words/s in English or 4.0 characters/s in Traditional Chinese. Unread story text is queued; production credits and URLs are filtered.

04

Speak and measure

Kokoro-82M (0.08B) speaks each draft and measures its real spoken duration. Overflows try 1.08× and 1.15× speed, then rewrite against the exact overflow, up to three rounds.

05

Gate and verify

Deterministic English and Mandarin AD rules block camera language, speculation and first person. A grounded verifier checks each claim against the shots on screen while it plays.

06

Describer Studio

gapcaster web opens a local-only (127.0.0.1) studio where describers audit frames and provenance, approve/edit/reject lines, and re-mix only approved lines in one click.

Every line carries its evidence: frames, earlier drafts, timing bar and flags. Open Mandarin review · Open English 8B review.
Why small models fit

Measure, don't guess

Audio description is a narrow craft task with a physical constraint: spoken duration must fit the silence between two lines of dialogue. By turning Kokoro-82M into a closed-loop duration sensor and enforcing AD style rules deterministically, an 8B model on one laptop hits 100% silence fit without calling a frontier API.

Small. Intentional. Tailored.

Small.

All core models are ≤10B open weights: Qwen3-VL-8B, Whisper large-v3-turbo (0.8B), Silero VAD and Kokoro-82M (0.08B) — 13.7–13.8 GB peak on one laptop, ~39–50 s per minute of video, zero cloud compute. Small studios and schools often handle unreleased or licensed footage that cannot be uploaded, on tiny budgets.

Intentional.

Footage, transcripts and drafts stay on 127.0.0.1: no accounts, no telemetry, no API calls. Every line keeps full JSON provenance, the describer's cast sheet governs naming, and export mixes only human-approved lines. Plain-text AD rules are open for describers and blind listeners to govern.

Tailored.

Built specifically around dialogue silences, professional AD guidelines, and a non-CLI browser review workflow. Tailored for Taiwan's AD gap with Traditional Chinese text tracks, character-rate budgets, Mandarin style rules, and an accessible screen-reader listening study.

Listener pilot: does it actually help?

We are testing the Mandarin and English described excerpts with blind and low-vision listeners and audio describers: 15 minutes, remote and anonymous, screen-reader friendly, no audio recording, two versions of the same scene.
