Isotherm

Each day it opens YES/NO markets on the Monad testnet for a city's high temperature, one per strike, and settles them from airport METAR reports through a Chainlink CRE workflow. A market maker quotes on Kuru order books, and AI agents can trade through a MetaMask Agent Wallet plugin. 149 Foundry tests.

Hardware prototype · Aug–Sep 2026

ESP32-S3 voice-agent smartwatch

A personal prototype that runs a voice AI agent on an ESP32-S3 AMOLED watch, built on xiaozhi-esp32 (MIT) and ESP-Claw (Apache-2.0) with my own Go voice gateway. When answers kept getting cut off, a control run ruled out speaker echo and traced the cause to barge-in with no confirmation window.
