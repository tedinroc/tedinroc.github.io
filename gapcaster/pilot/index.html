GapCaster · 口述影像聆聽測試Listening study

你聽得懂這個片段嗎？

Could you follow this film?

這是一場約 15 分鐘的線上無障礙聆聽測試，對象是盲人或低視力朋友。你會聽到同一段影片的兩個版本，然後回答幾個問題。沒有標準答案，也不需要稱讚我們。

This is a 15-minute screen-reader accessible listening study for blind and low-vision listeners. You will hear two versions of the same scene and answer a few questions. There are no right answers, and no need to be polite about our tool.

參與同意

Before you start

  • 這是什麼：測試 GapCaster 產生的口述影像（audio description）是否幫助盲／低視力觀眾跟上影片。研究由獨立開發者 Ted Chen（台灣）進行，作為 Mozilla Foundation Human-Scale AI Incubator 申請與開源工具改進之用。
  • 會發生什麼：你會在線上聽同一段約 2 分 20 秒的短片兩次（一個版本含口述影像，一個沒有）；聽完後回答 6 個 1 到 5 分評分題與 3 個開放題。全程約 15 分鐘，可隨時暫停或停止。
  • 不會發生什麼：我們不會錄音、不會錄影、不會收集你的姓名、電話或 email；不會公開你的姓名或逐字回答（經同意之匿名引述除外）。你只需要使用匿名編號（例如 P1）。
  • 資料怎麼用：你的評分與匿名引述可用於 Mozilla 補助申請與產品改進；原始回答在 90 天內刪除。你可以要求不要被引述。
  • 風險與補償：預設測試片段為台灣失智症協會人物紀實短片《Young Coffee Story》（CC BY 3.0，全程台灣國語訪談）；若切換為科幻短片《Tears of Steel》（CC BY 3.0）則含機器人與戰鬥音效。只聽聲音通常不會不適，你可以隨時停止。本測試不提供報酬（除非主持人另外說明）。
  • 聯絡：本測試主持人會提供聯絡方式；有問題可隨時提出。
  • What this is: A test of whether GapCaster's audio description helps blind and low-vision viewers follow a film. Run by independent developer Ted Chen (Taiwan) for a Mozilla Foundation Human-Scale AI Incubator application and open-source tool improvement.
  • What happens: You listen to the same 2 min 20 s excerpt twice online — one version with audio description, one without — then answer six 1-to-5 rating questions and three open questions. About 15 minutes. Pause or stop any time.
  • What does not happen: We do not record audio or video, and we do not collect your name, phone number or email. You only use an anonymous ID (for example, P1).
  • How data is used: Your ratings and anonymous quotes may be used in the Mozilla grant application and to improve the tool. Raw answers are deleted within 90 days. You can opt out of quotes below.
  • Risk and payment: Depending on the selected clip, the excerpt is either the Taiwanese Mandarin documentary short Young Coffee Story (Taiwan Alzheimer's Disease Association, CC BY 3.0) or the sci-fi short film Tears of Steel (Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0, with combat sounds). There is no payment unless the moderator says otherwise.
  • Contact: Your moderator will provide contact details; ask anything at any time.

先聽這兩個版本

Two versions of the same scene

兩個版本都是同一段 2 分 20 秒（0:00–2:20）的《Tears of Steel》。其中一個版本有中文口述影像，另一個沒有。口述是本機 8B 小模型產生的初稿，尚未經過口述影像員修改，聽到錯誤或彆扭的地方請直接告訴我們。每聽完一個版本，請先回答該版本的問題，再聽下一個。

Both versions are the same 2 min 20 s excerpt (0:00–2:20) of Tears of Steel by the Blender Foundation. One version has audio description fitted into the silences; the other does not. It is an unedited AI draft — please tell us about anything wrong or awkward. Answer each version's questions right after listening to it.
