GapCaster · 聆聽測試 / Listening study

你聽得懂這個片段嗎？

Could you follow this film?

這是一場約 15 分鐘的線上聆聽測試，對象是盲人或低視力朋友。你會聽到同一段電影的兩個版本，然後回答幾個問題。沒有標準答案，也不需要稱讚我們。

This is a 15-minute remote listening study for blind and low-vision listeners. You will hear two versions of the same scene and answer a few questions. There are no right answers, and no need to be polite about our tool.

參與同意

Before you start

  • 這是什麼：測試 GapCaster 產生的語音描述（audio description）是否幫助盲／低視力觀眾跟上影片。研究由獨立開發者 Ted Chen（台灣）進行，作為 Mozilla Foundation Human-Scale AI Incubator 申請與產品改進之用。
  • 會發生什麼：你會在線上聽同一段約 2 分 20 秒的電影，一個版本含語音描述，一個沒有；聽完後回答 6 個評分題與 3 個開放題。全程約 15 分鐘，可以隨時暫停或停止。
  • 不會發生什麼：我們不會錄音、不會錄影、不會收集你的姓名、電話或 email；不會公開你的姓名或逐字回答（匿名引述除外）。你只需要提供匿名編號（例如 P1）。
  • 資料怎麼用：你的評分與匿名引述可用於 Mozilla 補助申請與產品改進；原始回答在 90 天內刪除。你可以要求不要被引述。
  • 風險與補償：內容是科幻短片（Blender Foundation 的《Tears of Steel》，CC BY 3.0），含機器人、武器與戰鬥畫面。只聽聲音通常不會不適，但你可以隨時停止。本測試不提供報酬（除非主持人另外說明）。
  • 聯絡：本測試主持人會提供聯絡方式；有問題可隨時提出。
  • What this is: A test of whether GapCaster's audio description helps blind and low-vision viewers follow a film. The study is run by independent developer Ted Chen (Taiwan) for a Mozilla Foundation Human-Scale AI Incubator application and product improvement.
  • What happens: You listen to the same 2 min 20 s film excerpt twice online — one version with audio description, one without. Then you answer six rating questions and three open questions. About 15 minutes. You can pause or stop at any time.
  • What does not happen: We do not record audio or video, and we do not collect your name, phone number or email. You only use an anonymous ID (for example, P1).
  • How the data is used: Your ratings and anonymous quotes may be used in the Mozilla grant application and to improve the product. Raw answers are deleted within 90 days. You can ask not to be quoted.
  • Risk and payment: The excerpt is from the science-fiction short film Tears of Steel (Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0), which includes robots, weapons and combat. Listening is unlikely to be distressing, and you can stop at any time. There is no payment unless the moderator says otherwise.
  • Contact: The moderator will give you a contact. Ask anything, at any time.

先聽這兩個版本

Two versions of the same scene

兩個版本都是同一段 2 分 20 秒的片段（0:00–2:20），出自 Blender Foundation 的《Tears of Steel》。其中一個版本有語音描述（在對話空檔描述畫面），另一個沒有。片中對白是英文；口述預設是中文（可在下方切換成英文）。口述是 AI 產生的草稿，還沒有經過口述影像員修改，聽到錯誤或彆扭的地方請直接告訴我們。請依主持人指定的順序聆聽；每聽完一個版本，先回答該版本的問題，再聽下一個。聽完兩個版本後，還有一組比較題。

Both versions are the same 2 min 20 s excerpt (0:00–2:20) of Tears of Steel by the Blender Foundation. One version has audio description (narration fitted into the gaps between dialogue); the other does not. The film's dialogue is in English; the narration defaults to Mandarin (you can switch to English below). The narration is an AI draft that no describer has edited yet, so please tell us about anything wrong or awkward. Follow the order your moderator gives you: after each version, answer the questions for that version before moving to the next. After both, there is a short comparison section.
